Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What this app is

"Whyception" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Whyception YouTube channel (youtube.com/@whyception). It uploads the channel's own videos and reads the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel, and set their titles, descriptions, covers and publishing times;
  • read the channel's own numbers: views, likes, watch time, subscriber count, and the public comments on its videos.

What it collects about other people

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, no tracking and no advertising. It does not collect, sell or share personal information. Public comments on the channel's videos are read only so the owner can see them; they are not copied anywhere else or used for any other purpose.

Where the data is kept

The owner's YouTube authorisation and the channel's statistics are stored on the owner's own computer and private server, and are not shared with third parties.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website is two static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the channel's page: youtube.com/@whyception.
